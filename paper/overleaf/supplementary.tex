\noindent This supplement reports the prespecified sensitivity analyses and a post hoc allocation reference. All values are descriptive for the evaluated cohort and rules. The primary contrast remains the equal-capacity B4$-$B2 selective-risk difference at the 60\% target. B5/B6 were unavailable because the rule-selection partition contained six errors and 1{,}012 correct cases, below the prespecified 20/20 threshold.
